About SACFF

We are families from across San Antonio who gather each month to pray, open the Scriptures, worship and simply be together — because faith grows best in the company of others.

What began as a handful of families praying in a living room is now a fellowship spanning generations. There is no audition to belong here. Come as you are, bring the children, bring your questions, and find people who will walk with you.

  • Rooted in Scripture — every gathering opens the Word together.
  • Family first — children and youth are part of the room, not parked outside it.
  • Hands to serve — outreach across our city, all year round.
